## Do NOT claim

Each of these is either false, unverified, or a marketing claim dressed as a fact.

**Hard falsehoods — never write these**
1. **"$44.99"** or any Amazon price. The listing shows **"No featured offers available."** The $44.99 my scraper first picked up belongs to a *BabbleRoo* bag in the "customers also viewed" carousel. Never state a price sourced from that page.
2. **"Includes a power bank / battery / portable charger."** It does not. The port is pass-through only. A.E.'s review says so explicitly.
3. **"Waterproof."** Every source says **water resistant**. Do not upgrade the word.
4. **"13 pockets."** Contradicted by the manufacturer's own itemised graphic. If a number is used, use **15**, and be ready to show the breakdown.
5. **"Canvas."** No source says canvas. Amazon says nylon (weakly); reviewers say "heavy duty material." Prefer the reviewers' phrasing or say nothing.
6. **"Insulated bag" / "keeps the whole bag cold."** Only the 3 bottle holders and the separate bottle bag are insulated.
7. **"Available at Target / buybuybaby."** A founder statement from an undated interview; buybuybaby no longer trades. Do not present as current stockists.
8. **"Multiple colours" / "also in ___."** No colour variants exist on the listing.

**Unverified — no source reached, do not invent**
9. **Anything from the Etsy listing** — price, shop name, sales count, review count, shipping, processing time, description text, "handmade", "made in ___". Etsy was hard-blocked; **zero** Etsy facts were obtained.
10. **Weight, capacity in litres, laptop-sleeve size, warranty terms, return window, country of manufacture, fabric denier, care instructions.** None found anywhere.
11. **Interior lining colour/pattern.** The pinstripe lining appears only in adapted supplier artwork, never in a verified photo of this product.
12. **"7% of profits go to My Friend's House."** A 2019 founder statement. Do not print as a current, ongoing commitment without the founder re-confirming — a live donation claim on a commercial page is a legal exposure.
13. **"As seen on / endorsed by Eva Marcille / Porsha Williams."** They were *photographed with* the bag (Instagram, via SBP). That is not an endorsement, and the photos are not licensed. Do not imply sponsorship, and do not publish those two images without clearance.
14. **Anything about awards, press features, "featured in ___", or sales volume** beyond the six sources listed at the top.

**Framing cautions**
15. Do not write the founder as a dad. **As of the only interview on record he was not a father** — "I myself am not a father yet." The honest and far better story is that he built the bag he *wanted to carry* as a future father.
16. **"No two bags are the same"** is true-sounding and is the founder's own words, but it is his claim about how an all-over print gets cut. If used, quote and attribute it — don't assert it as a manufacturing spec.
17. The Amazon attribute table is seller-filled and partly garbage (`Pattern: Solid`, `Age Range: Kid`, `School Type: High School`, `Embellishment: embroidery`). Do not mine it for further "specs."
18. There are **no manufacturer feature bullets and no long-form product description** to draw on — the Amazon "description" is the title repeated. All persuasive copy must be built from the founder's interview and the customer reviews above, not from listing boilerplate.
