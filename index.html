Why it exists

It was fascinating to me to go to a b‑boy battle in Los Angeles and see all of the infants wearing colorful earmuffs being carried around by their parents. The next thought was — look at all of the amazing fathers hiphop has nurtured.

But where are the specific tools for the hiphop parent? How are we as a culture celebrating this space where parenthood and hiphop meet? Hip Hop Diaper Bag is my answer.

Marcus Gray / founder / Physics of Hiphop
